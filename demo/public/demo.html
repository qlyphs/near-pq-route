Each path ends at classical ed25519 keys. None of these protocols uses Guardian; the last card is the testnet token this page lets you try.

01

Two signers approve

Request 100 GDEMO for your account. Signers 0 and 1 sign the 320-byte operation; the third signer is not needed. The signatures are checked again in your browser.

02

Carry it to the chain

Your wallet signs a plain NEAR transaction that calls propose on the Guardian with the operation and its two approvals. Your key cannot change what was approved, only deliver it.

  1. Queued by the Guardian: two valid ML-DSA-87 approvals
  2. On-chain delay
  3. Execute: anyone can
  4. Settled on chain

Demo mode: this server holds signer keys 0 and 1 so that anyone can try it. In a real deployment each signer keeps its own key in its own wallet. Your NEAR wallet is a classical ed25519 key: it can carry and execute, never approve.
